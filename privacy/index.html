PlateSum Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

PlateSum keeps your data on your phone. It has no account, no servers of ours, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. This page explains the few cases where something leaves the device.

What the app stores

On your device only:

  • foods, products, recipes, your food journal and meal plans;
  • nutrition targets, declared allergies and excluded ingredients;
  • the country you shop in;
  • an optional profile: name, age, height and a dated history of your weight.

Allergies, targets and weight are health-related, and we treat them as sensitive. Every profile field is optional. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app, so this data is not copied to a cloud backup.

Barcode lookup

When you scan a barcode, the app sends that barcode over HTTPS to Open Food Facts, a free public food database. It asks the Open Food Facts site for the country you chose first (for example fr.openfoodfacts.org), then the world-wide site if needed. The site address therefore shows which country you chose. If you chose "Rest of the world", only the world-wide site is asked.

The request carries the barcode and the app's name and version. It carries nothing else: no allergies, targets, journal, profile, weight or device identifier. Open Food Facts handles requests under its own privacy policy.

A product is saved only after you confirm it. Scanning the same barcode again uses the saved copy and contacts no one.

Camera

The camera is used only for barcode scanning, after you start a scan. Images are processed on the device and are never saved or sent.

Feedback

Feedback in Settings opens your own mail app with your message and any photos or videos you pick, addressed to feedback@platesum.com. You decide whether to send it. We use what you send only to reply and to improve the app, and we delete it on request. The picked files are kept in the app's cache until you next open the feedback form.

Export and backup

Export and backup files are created only when you press the button, and you choose where they go. The files are not encrypted and can contain all your data, so keep them safe. Once you send a file to another app or service, that service's rules apply.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or AI services.

Deleting your data

Your data stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data (on Android: Settings, Apps, PlateSum, Storage, Clear data). Export files you saved elsewhere are not removed with the app; delete them yourself.

Children

PlateSum is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
