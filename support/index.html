PlateSum Support

Calories, macros and recipes. No account, everything stays on your phone.

Contact

Write to feedback@platesum.com, or use Feedback in the app's Settings to attach screenshots.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone, and on iPhone also in your own iCloud or computer backup if backups are on. PlateSum has no account and no servers, so we cannot see or recover your data. See the privacy policy.

Moving to a new phone

In Settings, open Data and backups and press Create database backup. Save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. There, install PlateSum and use Restore from a file. A backup replaces all data on the new phone.

A barcode is not found

Barcode data comes from Open Food Facts, a free public database, and not every product is in it. You can type the barcode instead of scanning, or enter the product's nutrition from the package yourself. Looking up a new barcode needs an internet connection; a product you saved works offline.

Allergens

Declare your allergies in Settings. PlateSum warns you when a food contains them, may contain them, or has no allergen data. It never shows unknown data as safe, but databases can be wrong: always read the package.

Not medical advice

PlateSum is not a medical device. Nutrition figures are estimates from food databases. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about your diet.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. All its data goes with it. An older iPhone backup keeps a copy until it is replaced. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.
